🌿 Remote Mower — Lawn Mower Platform BOM

Rev 1.1  ·  2026-03-17  ·  Target: 50 lb  ·  3.5–4 km/h on grass
Upgrade path from the test vehicle (bench prototype) to a real lawn mower platform. ♻️ Reused items carry over from the test vehicle BOM unchanged. 🔲 To Order items are new purchases for this platform. Brain: Arduino UNO Q 4GB (dual-brain: Qualcomm Linux + STM32 MCU).

Torque Justification

ParameterValue
Total platform weight~50 lbs (23 kg)
Grass rolling resistance coefficient0.25–0.3
Required drive force (total)23 kg × 9.8 × 0.3 = 68 N
Per motor (2 drive wheels)34 N
Target speed3.5–4 km/h (typical robotic mower pace)
Required RPM for 4 km/h4000m/h ÷ 60 ÷ (π × 0.2m) = ~106 RPM
Wheel radius (200 mm)0.1 m
Required torque per motor34 N × 0.1 m = ~3.4 N·m
Safety margin (2×)~5 N·m per motor
⚠️ Current JGA37-520 at 150 RPM produces ~0.7 N·m — 7× too weak for this application.
Speed check: 100 RPM × π × 0.2m wheel = 3.8 km/h ✅
